Orchestrator 
Natural-language requirements are parsed into a structured job descriptor. Only schema-validated 
tools may be invoked. Each phase returns logs, quantitative metrics and a retry flag. Replanning 
addresses (1) geometric/meshing anomalies, (2) numerical solver aborts and (3) physical limit-
state violations. Backends include FreeCAD, Gmsh, CalculiX–BESO, SQP upscaling, PSO sizing 
and Zwind 2.5. Termination requires inactive retry flags, sizing convergence with zero maximum 
limit-state violation, and Automated Reviewer **S ≥ 85** with all sub-scores ≥60 (weights 0.15, 
0.30, 0.25, 0.15, 0.15 for capacity, steel intensity, unit cost, constructability, fatigue). Artifacts are 
hashed (SHA-256) for audit. 
Topology, upscaling and sizing 
BESO minimizes compliance under a volume fraction with soft-kill void stiffness and spatial 
filtering (Supplementary Methods). Anisotropic fixed-draft upscaling scales horizontal 
dimensions by a power-law factor refined by SQP under pitch constraints. PSO minimizes steel 
mass subject to tilt, unity check ≤1.0, 1P/3P avoidance and fatigue limits; virtual-work 
sensitivities reduce full FEA calls (Supplementary Methods). 
Zwind 
Zwind 2.5 integrates aero-hydro-servo-elastic coupling via Lagrange equations for rigid–flexible 
multibody systems (Supplementary Note). Public reproduction packages include solver input 
decks and structural subsets; full proprietary Zwind binaries follow their licenses.
