# Story research / 2026-10-10

Read the full accessible text of:

- US Department of Energy, *Floating Offshore Wind Shot*: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/floating-offshore-wind-shot . Explains why deeper waters need floating foundations, and how cost, reliability and deployment are linked. Used only to frame the engineering problem; its programme targets are not the paper's performance results.
- Formlabs, *Topology Optimization 101*: https://formlabs.com/blog/topology-optimization/ . Read the bracket/manufacturing explanation and the FEM workflow. The useful narrative is to introduce material and loads before naming an algorithm. Its marketing simplifications about low-load removal, resonance and error elimination are not adopted as scientific claims.

The film uses an original problem chain: wind/waves/mooring → conflicting weight and stiffness requirements → design-domain discretization → BESO material redistribution → reconstructable members → dynamic tradeoffs → evidence and review. All quantitative results come from versioned local paper/run files, not these introductory web pages. No social-media engagement figures or claims of having watched unseen videos are made.

Alibaba Cloud documentation was also fetched for the native DashScope media API. Generated transitions are conceptual images and carry no numerical data or certification claim. Data and model endpoints remain the local deterministic render.
